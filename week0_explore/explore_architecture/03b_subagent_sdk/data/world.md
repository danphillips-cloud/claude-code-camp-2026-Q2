## Dungeon Below Alchemist's Room (Extensively Explored)
**Entry:** Down stairway from The Alchemist's Room  
**Critical Path:** Alchemist's Room → dark stairway down → complex dungeon passages → RED ROOM (where MINOTAUR is contained)

**Navigation Hazards:**
- Multiple pitch-black areas requiring navigation without lighting
- Closed doors throughout (some open with "open door" command, some remain blocked)
- Confusing room names that repeat (multiple "Large Rooms", "Another Turn", "Passages")
- Easy to become disoriented; agent got lost in darkness

**Red Room Location (MINOTAUR LOCATION):**
- Contains: Round red portal on floor depicting dirt field
- Phenomenon: Realistic wind blowing from the portal
- Exit: Down from Red Room leads back to Great Field
- Creatures: Multiple quasits present
- Status: **MASSIVE MINOTAUR CONFIRMED TO BE HERE** (per user intel)

**Mapped Rooms:**
- The Entrance, Turn In The Passage, Branch To The South, Large Room(s), Passage, Crossing Of Corridors, Corner In The Hallway, Another Turn (multiple), Branching Passage, The Red Room, Intersection Of Passages, Yet Another Corner, A Narrow Passage

**Creatures Encountered:**
- Quasits (imp-like demons) - multiple locations, appear harmless/neutral
- Zombie-like newbies (gaunt, dangerous)
- Pet dragon (loose, avoided)
- Gelatinous blob (bar area, NOT dungeon) - EXTREME THREAT (one-shot 23 damage to level 1)

**Loot Found:**
- Gelatinous blob corpse (not looted, fled combat)
- Creepy crawler corpse (looted: 10 gold coins)
- Equipment scattered at inn (staff, sword, wristguards, belt, cape, shield, sleeves, gloves, boots, leggings, cap, breastplate, neck guards, rings)
